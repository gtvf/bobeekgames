Privacy Policy

Podstakannik Rails — last updated 30 August 2026

Podstakannik Rails is developed and published by Bobeek Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your data when you play the game. It is short because the game collects very little.

We run no servers and keep no database

Multiplayer sessions are hosted by one of the players and connect through Steam. We do not operate game servers, we do not receive your data, and we do not store anything about you on our side.

What is processed while you play

  • Your Steam ID and Steam display name. Used to place you in a lobby and to show who is who. Other players in your session can see your display name above your character. This is handled by Steam; it is required for multiplayer to work at all.
  • Your voice, while you hold the push-to-talk key. Voice chat is provided by Vivox, part of Unity Gaming Services. Your audio is transmitted to the other players in your session in real time. We do not record, store, or listen to it, and neither the game nor we keep any copy. Your Steam ID and display name are passed to Vivox so voices can be matched to players.
  • Your settings and character choices. Volume levels, graphics options, key bindings, chosen language, and your character's appearance are saved on your own computer through Unity's PlayerPrefs. They never leave your device and are never sent to us.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No accounts to create and no passwords — the game uses your existing Steam session.
  • No payment information reaches us; all purchases are handled by Steam.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with anyone.

Third parties

Two services are involved in running the game, each under its own privacy policy:

  • Valve Corporation (Steam) — distribution, ownership, friends, lobbies and matchmaking. See the Steam Privacy Policy.
  • Unity Technologies (Vivox / Unity Gaming Services) — voice chat. Unity processes the voice stream and may collect technical identifiers needed to operate the service. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from them. Because voice chat is not moderated or filtered, parents should be aware that players can hear each other speak freely.

Your rights

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing on our side to export or delete. Requests concerning your Steam account should go to Valve, and requests concerning voice service data to Unity, using the policies linked above. If you are unsure where to ask, write to us and we will point you in the right direction.

Changes

If the game starts processing anything else, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the date at the top will change.
